## R2. Year typos set to the survey year

A record whose date-year differs from its `survey_year` is a year typo when its month-day falls inside the window of correctly dated records of the same beach-year (`window_first` to `window_last`). Such dates have the year replaced by `survey_year` (`date_year_corrected = TRUE`). Records failing the test are kept as recorded and flagged in `date_flag`. 711 of 711 cross-year records were corrected.
