# docs/references

Primary documents deposited with the repository because they are not available elsewhere. Published references are cited in the manuscript with DOIs and are not stored here.

| File | What it is | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| `Cheng_Ayres_2008_razor_clam_growth_WDFW_draft.doc` | Henry Cheng and Dan Ayres (Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife), *Modelling the growth of razor clam (Siliqua patula) in Washington State coast*. Unpublished working draft; Word file created 18 January 2008, last saved 1 February 2008 (file metadata; the document names no author). Fits von Bertalanffy curves to 2006–2007 mark-recapture data at Copalis and Long Beach by the random-effects method of Cheng and Kuk (2002, *Biometrics* 58:459–462). Its Table 2 is the source of the growth parameters used in this repository (Copalis L∞ 143.17 mm, K 1.00 yr⁻¹; Long Beach 141.67 mm, 0.98 yr⁻¹) | Supplied by the repository owner (Matthew George, WDFW) on 2 October 2026; authorship confirmed and permission to deposit given by the owner on 17 October 2026 |
| `Cheng_Ayres_2008_razor_clam_growth_WDFW_draft.txt` | Machine text extraction of the above (antiword), for searching and for agents without Word. Embedded equations and figures appear as `[pic]`; the `.doc` is authoritative | Made 2 October 2026 |

How the draft is used, and what it does and does not establish (K and L∞ yes, the age origin t₀ no), is in `docs/growth-model-review.md`. Cite it as:

> Cheng YW, Ayres D (2008) Modelling the growth of razor clam (*Siliqua patula*) in Washington State coast. Unpublished draft, Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife, Olympia. Deposited at `docs/references/` in this repository.
